# Weerbaar-Resources
A minecraft resourcepack for me and my friends.

## WARNING
### CONTENT
This pack contains profanity and inside jokes. Any and all refences to extremist ideology or organisations, such as Schild en Vrienden, are purely satirical and not to be taken as my believes, political or otherwise, nor as my endorsement.
Me and my friends enjoy making fun of these people, and thus much of their lingo has made their way into our vocabulary as a way of ironic usage.
### DEPENDENCIES
I try to keep it vanilla friendly, but this pack was made to be used with Optifine or mods that support Optifine features.
### BRANCHES
The 1.20.4 branch is the one I actively work on, the others are ports from previous versions.
